  ### Summary of the Flow

  1. User logs in.
  2. Frontend calls  GET /api/system/ups-state  and  GET /api/system/consumo-actual .
  3. The dashboard UI renders:
      • Green dial (Grid) or Amber/Red dial (Battery) based on  modo_actual .
      • The master power consumption using  potencia_total_w .
      • Autonomy computations based on the 1200W inverter and 2x12V 9Ah battery specs.
  4. If the power cuts, the backend pushes a  gateway_alerta  ->  UPS_BATTERY_MODE  WebSocket event.
  5. The frontend catches it, updates the store, and the UI immediately reflects that the house is now
  drawing power from the batteries.
